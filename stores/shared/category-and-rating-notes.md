# Category and age rating: suggestions for the owner to confirm

These are suggestions only. The owner confirms them in App Store Connect and Play Console.

## Category
- **App Store primary category:** Shopping.
- **App Store secondary category (optional):** Lifestyle.
- **Google Play category:** Shopping.
- **Google Play tags:** pick the classifieds / buy-and-sell tags that the Console offers under Shopping.

## Age
- **Minimum age:** 13, from `docs/production/READINESS.md` (13+ Terms and age agreement).
- **Store ratings:** the questionnaires decide the rating, and READINESS notes it "may be higher" than 13. Answer them honestly:
  - users talk to each other in chat (photos, locations, offers);
  - users post their own content (listings, photos);
  - location is shared only when a user sends a pin or picks a city;
  - there are no in-app purchases and no in-app payments.
- **Google Play content rating (IARC):** the answers above will likely put the app at Teen or 12+. The exact result depends on the answers.
- **Apple age rating:** the user-generated content and messaging answers set the result.
- **Under-18 users:** READINESS also asks for Apple Declared Age Range and Google Play Age Signals with parental consent for ages 13 to 17 before US availability.

## Store review notes
READINESS asks for these in the review notes:
- the security check (CAPTCHA);
- listing moderation;
- report, block and delete;
- banned-goods enforcement;
- the 24-hour response target.

The store descriptions leave out the 24-hour target. It is still an open launch gate, so it is not a public promise yet. Add it to the descriptions only after it is published and staffed.
