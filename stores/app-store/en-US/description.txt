hemû is a free marketplace app for Kurdistan. Buy and sell things near you — from Hewlêr to Amed — in your own language.

Post in a minute
• Take photos with your camera or choose them from your gallery
• Pick a category, set your price and your location
• Buyers can see your listing as soon as you publish it

Find exactly what you want
• Browse categories: vehicles, real estate, electronics and appliances, home and garden, fashion, and more
• Choose your city, several locations, or all of Kurdistan
• Search, use filters and sort by price
• Save a search and get an alert when a new listing matches

Agree on a price in chat
• Message the seller straight from the listing
• Send photos and share a location on the map
• Make an offer; the other person can accept it or send a counter-offer
• No payment is made in hemû. You agree between you how to pay and where to meet.

Keep track of what you like
• Tap the heart to save listings to your favourites
• Follow the sellers you like and see their new listings on Home
• See prices in US dollars, Iraqi dinars, Turkish lira, Syrian pounds or Iranian toman (converted prices are an estimate)

Six languages
Kurdî (Kurmancî), کوردی (سۆرانی), English, Türkçe, العربية, فارسی. Choose your language when you open the app.

A respectful marketplace
• Report a listing or a chat, and block anyone you don't want to hear from
• Our team reviews reports. Illegal and banned items are not allowed.
• You must be 13 or older to use hemû.
• You can delete your account from inside the app.

Tip: meet in a public place and check the item before you pay.

You can browse without an account. Sign in to post, send messages and save searches.
